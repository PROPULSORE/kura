# Kura

Kura is a free app for **Android and Windows** that keeps your anime, manga,
light novels, TV series and movies in one library, and lets you watch episodes
and read chapters in the same place.

- One library with progress, statuses and statistics.
- Video player and manga and light novel reader.
- Extensions that read the websites you choose (Kura comes without any).
- Optional sync with AniList, MyAnimeList and Trakt.
- Backup and sync through your own Google Drive or your own server, end-to-end
  encrypted.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Kura is in **beta**.
